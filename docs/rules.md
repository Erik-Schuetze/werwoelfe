# The Werewolves of Millers Hollow (Game Rules)

THIS FILE IS IMMUTABLE NEVER CHANGE ITS CONTENT AS THESE ARE THE OFFICIAL RULES

## 1. Overview and Objectives
* **Players:** 8 to 18 players (+ 1 non-playing Moderator).
* **Setting:** The small village of Millers Hollow is infiltrated by werewolves. Each night, the werewolves claim a victim. Each day, the remaining villagers attempt to unmask and execute the werewolves.
* **Victory Conditions:**
  * **Townsfolk:** Win immediately when all Werewolves are eliminated.
  * **Werewolves:** Win immediately when all Townsfolk are eliminated.
  * **Lovers (Special Case):** If the two Lovers consist of one Werewolf and one Townsfolk, their objective changes: they win only if all other players are eliminated.

---

## 2. Roles and Abilities

### Werewolves
* **Werewolf (1–4):** Each night, all Werewolves wake together and silently agree on one victim to devour. During the day, they conceal their identity and participate in discussions as ordinary villagers.

### Townsfolk
* **Ordinary Townsfolk / Villager:** Possesses no special abilities. Uses observation, deduction, and voting to identify and eliminate the Werewolves.
* **Fortune Teller (Seer):** Wakes up each night before the Werewolves to view the secret identity card of any one player chosen by pointing.
* **Hunter:** If eliminated by Werewolves, the Witch, or the village vote, the Hunter immediately fires a final shot, choosing any one player who is also instantly eliminated.
* **Witch:** Possesses two single-use potions for the entire game:
  * *Healing Potion:* Revives the victim chosen by the Werewolves that night (can be used on herself).
  * *Poison Potion:* Eliminates any one player of her choice.
  * She may use zero, one, or both potions in a single night.
* **Cupid:** Wakes only on the preliminary first night to shoot his arrows at two players (can include himself). These two become Lovers. If one Lover dies, the other immediately dies of heartbreak. Lovers may never vote against each other.
* **Little Girl:** During the Werewolf phase at night, she may secretly peek through half-closed eyes. If caught by the Werewolves, she can be eliminated immediately instead of or as their victim.
* **Thief:** Added only when using the Thief variant. Two extra Ordinary Townsfolk cards are placed face-down in the center of the table. The Thief wakes during the preliminary night to view both cards and may swap their Thief card for one of them. If both center cards are Werewolves, the swap is mandatory.

### Additional Role
* **Sheriff (Captain):** Conferred in addition to a player's secret role card by majority vote on the first day. This title cannot be refused.
  * The Sheriff's vote counts as 2 during village trials.
  * Upon elimination, the Sheriff names their successor with their dying breath.

## 4. Turn Structure

### Phase 0: Setup
1. Appoint a Moderator to narrate and manage the game.
2. Deal one secret role card face-down to each player.
3. Players secretly look at their cards and place them face-down.
4. If playing with the Thief, place two extra Ordinary Townsfolk cards face-down in the center.

### Phase 1: Preliminary Night (First Night Only)
1. **Nightfall:** The Moderator announces: *"Night falls over the village, everyone falls asleep."* All players close their eyes and lower their heads.
2. **Thief (if in play):** Moderator wakes the Thief. The Thief views the two center cards and may swap roles (must swap if both are Werewolves). The Thief goes back to sleep.
3. **Cupid (if in play):** Moderator wakes Cupid. Cupid points to two players to become Lovers. Cupid goes back to sleep.
4. **Lovers:** The Moderator walks around the table and taps both Lovers on the shoulder. The Moderator announces: *"The Lovers wake, recognize each other, and go back to sleep."* The two open their eyes, make eye contact without revealing their exact roles, and go back to sleep.

### Phase 2: Recurring Night Phase
1. **The Fortune Teller:** Wakes, points to one player. The Moderator silently reveals that player's card to her. The Fortune Teller goes back to sleep.
2. **The Werewolves:** Wake, make eye contact, and silently designate one victim by consensus. (The Little Girl may attempt to peek during this time). The Werewolves go back to sleep.
3. **The Witch:** Wakes. The Moderator silently shows her the Werewolves' victim. The Witch indicates whether to use her Healing Potion (thumb up) and/or her Poison Potion (thumb down pointing to a target). The Witch goes back to sleep.

### Phase 3: Day Phase
1. **Dawn:** The Moderator announces: *"The sun rises, everyone wakes up except..."* and points to the night's victim(s).
   * Victims flip their cards face-up and are eliminated. Eliminated players cannot speak, vote, or signal in any way.
   * If the Hunter died, he immediately names and eliminates another player.
   * If a Lover died, the other Lover immediately dies and reveals their card.
   * If the Sheriff died, they name a successor.
2. **Sheriff Election (Day 1 only):** The village elects a Sheriff by relative majority.
3. **Discussion:** The living players discuss who among them might be a Werewolf. Bluffing and role claims are permitted.
4. **Village Vote (Execution):**
   * On the Moderator's signal, every player points simultaneously at the player they wish to execute.
   * The player with the most votes is executed, reveals their card, and is eliminated.
   * The Sheriff's vote counts as 2.
   * **Tiebreaker:** If there is a tie, the Sheriff breaks it (if they voted for one of the tied players). If not, a revote is held between only the tied players. If a second tie occurs, no one is executed that day.
5. **Dusk:** The Moderator announces nightfall, and the game returns to Phase 2.

---

## 5. End of Game
The game ends immediately when any victory condition is fulfilled:
* **All Werewolves are dead:** Townsfolk win.
* **All Townsfolk are dead:** Werewolves win.
* **Mixed Lover pair is the only surviving duo:** Lovers win.
